Sunset Cover Studio turns your photos and a caption into GTA-style cover art. Choose a multi-panel layout — Stack, Grid or Strip — drag each photo to crop and place it, write your caption, pick a Vice City sunset colour grade, and export a PNG at 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080. The whole thing runs offline in the browser, so your photos never leave your machine. The poster is painted on canvas: a retro slatted sun, palm silhouettes, and big condensed Anton lettering (SIL OFL). An original design — no Rockstar logo, artwork or font anywhere.

## How it works

1. Add up to three photos from your machine, or try the built-in samples.
2. Pick a layout and assign a photo to each panel — one photo can fill several panels.
3. Drag photos inside their panels to crop them, zoom with the sliders, and type your caption.
4. Choose a colour grade (Sunset, Neon, Dusk or Noir) and an export size, then hit Export PNG.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — add photos (or try the samples), pick a layout and grade, and hit Export PNG.
