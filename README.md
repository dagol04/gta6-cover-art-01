Sunset Cover Studio makes GTA-style cover art from your photos and a caption. Pick a multi-panel layout (Stack, Grid, Strip), drag each photo to crop and place it, write your caption, choose a Vice City sunset colour grade, and export a PNG at 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080. It runs fully offline in the browser, so photos never leave your machine. The cover is painted on canvas — a retro slatted sun, palm silhouettes, and big condensed Anton lettering (SIL OFL) — an original design with no Rockstar logo, artwork or font.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — add photos (or try the samples), pick a layout and grade, and hit Export PNG.
