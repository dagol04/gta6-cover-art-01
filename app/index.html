Sunset Cover Studio

Turn your photos and a caption into multi-panel GTA-style cover art. It runs entirely offline in your browser — your photos never leave your machine — and the PNG exports straight from the canvas.

1 · Photos

Add up to three of your photos, or try a sample. Any panel can show any of them.

2 · Layout

Stack puts one hero photo over two. Grid makes four panels. Strip makes three tall panels.

3 · Panels

Click a panel in the preview to pick it, then drag its photo around. Zoom and swap photos here.

4 · Words
5 · Colour grade

Sunset is warm, Neon is loud, Dusk is violet, Noir is black and white.

6 · Export

Live preview — click a panel to select it, drag to move its photo. The PNG matches what you see.
